# ClawForge Diagnostic Offers

Practical, fixed-scope diagnostics for teams that need clearer operational evidence before committing to a larger automation project.

## Choose the problem you need to solve

### Agent Reliability Diagnostic

For teams running tool-using AI agents that loop, duplicate actions, recover ambiguously, or lack enforceable spend and terminal-state controls.

- **USD 250 narrow triage:** one sanitized failure trace and one prioritized acceptance-test recommendation
- **USD 500 standard diagnostic:** terminal states, duplicate actions, retry/recovery, spend limits, evidence trail, and prioritized repair plan
- No credentials, secrets, or private customer data are required for the initial review

[View the Agent Reliability offer](https://anitsap-creator.github.io/weekly-intelligence-brief-agent/agent-reliability/)

### Missed Lead Recovery Audit

For plumbing, roofing, HVAC, and other home-service teams that cannot reliably see which inbound leads were missed, delayed, or left without an owner.

- **USD 299 fixed scope**
- Maps intake and follow-up gaps
- Produces a sample priority queue and exception-report design
- Recommends the smallest useful alerting or workflow change

[View the Missed Lead Recovery offer](https://anitsap-creator.github.io/weekly-intelligence-brief-agent/missed-lead/)

### Weekly Intelligence Brief

For SEO, content, and SaaS-growth agencies that need recurring evidence about competitor, category, SERP, pricing, proof, or offer movement.

- **USD 149 two-week pilot**
- Monitors 3–5 public sources
- Produces two evidence-backed briefs
- Includes prior-week comparison and recommended actions

[View the Weekly Intelligence Brief offer](https://anitsap-creator.github.io/weekly-intelligence-brief-agent/)

## How it works

1. Choose the offer matching the operational problem.
2. Share a short, non-sensitive description when contact is available.
3. Scope and fit are confirmed before payment or access is requested.
4. Delivery stays within the fixed scope unless both sides explicitly agree otherwise.

## Evidence boundary

The samples on the offer pages demonstrate the format and evaluation method. They are not customer testimonials or claims of recovered revenue, guaranteed reliability, leads, or business outcomes.

The initial review does not require credentials, production access, private customer data, login/paywall bypass, or private scraping.
